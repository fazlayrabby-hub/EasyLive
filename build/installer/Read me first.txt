EasyLive - Read me first

EasyLive by Sohan Roni. Share your screen or a window live on YouTube or Facebook.

Quick start (5 steps)
 1. Open EasyLive from the Start menu (or the desktop shortcut).
 2. Pick what to share: a whole screen, or one window.
 3. Choose YouTube or Facebook.
 4. Paste your stream key. Find it in YouTube Studio ("Go live", then "Stream")
    or in Facebook Live Producer ("Streaming software"). Click "Where do I find this?" in the app for help.
 5. Press the big red Go Live button. When you are finished, press Stop,
    then end the broadcast on YouTube or Facebook too.

Good to know
 - The first time you use YouTube, it can take up to 24 hours before YouTube lets you stream.
 - Keep your stream key private. Anyone with it can stream to your channel.
 - Your key is saved only on this computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EasyLive.
 - If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen when you first run Setup,
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". This happens because EasyLive is not code-signed yet.
 - If Windows Firewall asks, allow EasyLive so it can reach the internet.
